SongBridge Stories integration — November 16, 2026

This is an UPDATE for the existing songbridgelearning.com repository, not a complete replacement website or standalone Netlify drop.

1. Unzip this package.
2. Open the GitHub repository connected to your existing songbridgelearning.com Netlify site.
3. At the repository's top level, use Add file > Upload files.
4. Drag in index.html, app.js, songbridge-stories.css, and the entire stories folder together. Keep the stories folder intact; do not upload its contents at the repository's top level.
5. Commit the changes. Allow the existing Netlify deployment to finish.
6. Open songbridgelearning.com and choose Song stories. Check the homepage Read the story link, a Behind this song link, a language cover choice, and Copy story link.

Keep all other existing repository files and folders, including audio, covers, art, timings, scripts, and styles. No Netlify settings or domain changes are needed.

Contents: revised homepage and app script, new stylesheet, stories/index.html, artwork, and icons. Includes 18 stories and links from 23 matching recordings. Songs without a story retain their existing controls.

The stories retain their original wording, artwork, audio and lyrics. The public page adds story-link copying and a route back to the song collection; authoring guidance and the separate HTML-download control are omitted from this integrated edition. Print/PDF and Copy introduction remain available.

Prepared from the live homepage and app.js retrieved November 16, 2026, and the SongBridge Stories project's fourth source version. This package has not been deployed to songbridgelearning.com.
