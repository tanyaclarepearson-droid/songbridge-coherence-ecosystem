SongBridge v7 — selected songs in your personal studio

FOR: the existing songbridgelearning.com repository.

Update Personal Studio to v27 first. Merge all included website files and the art/stories folders into your EXISTING SongBridge repository. Keep every existing audio, lyrics, data, image, and other file not included here. Do not replace the whole repository or deploy this partial patch as a standalone site.

New files required: resource-contract.js, resource-send.js, resource-send.css. Updated entry files: index.html, app.js. Existing v6 navigation, story pages, player, and art are retained.

On a song, choose Bring into my studio. Review/edit its title, language, moment, audience and suggested use, then choose Review in my studio. Sign in there and explicitly Save to my connected shelf. The hub stores a private song card and a link to that exact song; lyrics and audio remain in SongBridge. Re-sending the same song replaces its earlier card.

If the handoff window is blocked, download the selected resource here, then import it under Connected apps in Personal Studio. Sign in before receiving a card, or download it before using an email sign-in link.

Validation: song selection test and syntax checks pass. Shared sender tests pass in Talk Tuner; hub receiver and database contract tests pass. Full live-song playback and the signed-in phone journey were not retested here. This patch preserves the original supplied media/art files and does not bundle the entire live site's audio/data.
